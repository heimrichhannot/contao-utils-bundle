SearchVectorQuery
in package
  • Application

SearchVectorQuery.php : 23

Builds search conditions against the search vector of a table.

Query syntax: commas separate alternatives (OR), the words of one alternative must all match (AND), words wrapped in double quotes must match as a phrase. Words match as substrings of the stored stems, so compounds are found by their parts ("Württemberg" finds "Baden-Württemberg").

The search is stemmed in the language the records were indexed in. For tables with a language field it defaults to the language of the current request, pass $locale to search in another language.

Matches can be sorted by relevance: a word at the start of a word scores 1, a word that is a whole word 1 more, and the words of an alternative standing next to each other in the order of the search score 2 per word on top. Words of quoted phrases count individually. So "Dresdner Stollen" ranks records with both words in a row before records with the words in different places, and "Honig" ranks "Honig" before "Imkerhonig".
